## 지도 프롬프트

Create an original detailed painted Korean wuxia regional world map game background, landscape 16:9. Traditional East Asian ink and mineral pigment landscape painting on warm aged handmade mulberry paper, delicate brushwork, layered jade/ochre mountain relief, pine forests, hamlets, walled mountain strongholds, fine riverbanks, cloud wisps, atmospheric miniature diorama terrain. Entire frame one continuous map with NO text, NO lettering, NO icons, NO UI, NO map markers, NO drawn routes; these will be overlaid by game code. Composition for precise existing game waypoints (percent across image, percent down image): eight mountain fortresses whose gates sit around (12.5%,52%),(31%,80%),(27%,26%),(46%,58%),(76%,79%),(73%,51%),(53%,18%),(86%,18%). Fortress paintings extend above their gate coordinates leaving gate vicinity clear for marker labels. Village at (12%,83%); courier compound at (43%,91%); river ferry at (56%,83%); manor at (88%,77%); eastern pass at (95%,45%). River winds from (61%,40%) through (55%,76%) toward bottom right. Rich impressive genuinely hand-painted terrain throughout, tiny roof details and varying mountain formations, not a flat vector diagram, not geometric repeated triangles, not photorealistic. Leave unobtrusive open lowland connections between places. Soft luminous parchment edges. No copied copyrighted names or logos. Production art asset.

## 종이 프롬프트

One single blank old East Asian handmade paper sheet texture for an elegant game UI nine-slice panel. Landscape 3:2 rectangle filling the entire canvas edge-to-edge, no surroundings. Flat front-facing scan, softly uneven worn edges within the outermost 3 percent, warm pale ivory cream central 85 percent with very subtle visible mulberry fibers, small faded amber water stains near margins, delicately darker brown rim. Minimal quiet center to ensure dark text legibility. No writing no symbols no objects no rolled scroll handles no inset panels no borders within the sheet, not a collage, not scorched dark, not yellow saturated. Fully opaque. Detailed high-resolution paper material, no perspective.
